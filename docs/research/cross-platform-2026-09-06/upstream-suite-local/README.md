# Local upstream-suite and Darwin exit-code evidence

The initial .NET 10 suite passed 29 of 30 scenarios. The required exit-code test failed and blocked the candidate. After the two Darwin waitpid entry points were connected through the external library, targeted installed-debugger runs passed on .NET 8 and .NET 10. Paths in the archived transcripts are normalized; original and archived hashes are recorded separately. This is local experimental evidence, not an eight-platform candidate result.
